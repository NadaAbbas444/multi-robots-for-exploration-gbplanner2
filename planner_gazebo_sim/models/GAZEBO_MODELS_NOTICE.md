# Third-party Gazebo models

The folders `bus`, `fire_truck`, `pickup`, `suv`, `construction_cone`,
`construction_barrel` and `jersey_barrier` are unmodified copies from the
Gazebo model database, https://github.com/osrf/gazebo_models
(commit 8163eb4b5e7e21985c6591d1c0bfb56468c0093f), licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported — see `GAZEBO_MODELS_LICENSE`.
They are used in `worlds/construction_tunnel.world`.
